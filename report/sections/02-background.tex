\subsection{Reaction--diffusion systems}
\label{sec:rd-systems}

\todo{General form, then the specific model you choose.}

A two-species reaction--diffusion system has the form
\begin{equation}
	\label{eq:rd-general}
	\pd{u}{t} = f(u, v) + \Du \lap u, \qquad
	\pd{v}{t} = g(u, v) + \Dv \lap v,
\end{equation}
where $f$ and $g$ describe the local chemistry and the $\lap$ terms describe diffusion.
