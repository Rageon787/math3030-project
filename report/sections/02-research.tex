% General Explanation of Reaction Diffusion System
This project explores a system of partial differential equations known as Reaction Diffusion Systems. Reaction Diffusion Systems emerge from chemistry and are used as a tool to model how substances from a chemical reaction *diffuse* in space. Hence, the name Reaction Diffusion Systems.

% Research from "Complex Patterns in a Simple System"
I just briefly read a paper called Complex patterns in simple system by J.E Pearson. He showed that numerical simulation on a simple reaction diffusion system in a 2 dimensional space can produce irregular spatio temporal patterns ranging from Regular Hexagon to Chaotic spatio temporal patterns. The author introduces the Gray Scott Model (Which I learned just yesterday from that video), provides a bifurcation analysis and does a numerical simulation using forward euler and discretisation to produce these patterns.

The simple system described by Pearson is the Gray Scott Model.
