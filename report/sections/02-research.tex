\subsection{Reaction Diffusion Systems Model Chemical Reactions}
% What phenomenon do they model 
A *Reaction Diffusion System* describes a chemical reaction of two or more substances and how they diffuse with respect to time and space. In chemistry, diffusion represents a substance moving from a higher concentration region to a lower concentration region.

The diffusion is given by the Laplacian $D \delta^2$. Mathematically, it is the average of neighbours.

\subsection{Simple RDS model: Gray Scott}

The Gray-Scott Model is an example of a Reaction Diffusion System that models the reaction of two chemicals $A$ and $B$ based on a specific rule $A + 2B \to 3B$. Numerical Simulations on the model using a method known as Forward Euler Integration have been shown to produce *irregular spatio temporal patterns \cite{pearson1993}.

	\partial B & = AB
