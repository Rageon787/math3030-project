\subsection{Gray-Scott Model}

The Gray-Scott Model is an example of a Reaction Diffusion System that models the reaction of two chemicals $A$ and $B$ based on a specific rule $A + 2B \to 3B$. Numerical Simulations on the model using a method known as Forward Euler Integration have been shown to produce *irregular spatio temporal patterns \cite{pearson1993}.

\begin{align*}
	\partial A & = AB \\
	\partial B & = AB
\end{align*}

- A has a feed rate
- B has a kill rate
- Both chemicals have some diffusion rate that is represented by diffusion constant.
- Similar to a prey and predator model but with one extension, A is like food for Bs that produce three Bs
